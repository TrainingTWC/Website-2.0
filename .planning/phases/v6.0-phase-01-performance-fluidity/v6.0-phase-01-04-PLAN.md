<objective>
The three heaviest visual effects on the site are the 3D bestseller carousel (Framer Motion springs + perspective + lots of motion.div), the R3F `ProductHero3D` Canvas, and the always-on `MagneticCursor`. `GalaxySweep` fires its particle burst on transitions. None of them are essential on a low-spec device. Wrap each behind the tier signal so capable devices get the show and weak devices get a fast, still-good experience.

Purpose: A 4 GB Chromebook should not be downloading three.js and running a 60fps spring loop just to scroll the home page.

Output: A new flat carousel fallback component, tier-gated wrappers around all four effects.
</objective>
